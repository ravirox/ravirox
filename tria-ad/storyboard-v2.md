# Tria Federal brand ad: storyboard v2

**Tagline:** Powering the Systems Behind Federal Health.
**Plan:** Story 2 ("The Trilemma") is the 60-second hero ad. Story 1 ("Behind Every Moment") is the 30-second social cutdown.

This version adds the decisions made in review:
- **Footage:** real-looking video throughout. Stock footage comes first; Higgsfield fills only the gaps. No still images.
- **Settings:** real and lived-in, but balanced, with 2–3 believable details per scene and no clutter.
- **One look:** every shot is lit and colour-graded to the same standard (section 1). The approved images guide the look; they are not used in the ad.
- **The red line:** one continuous, wide, flowing red light trail runs through the whole ad. It is built as an animated graphic in editing and timed to the voiceover (section 2). It is never generated inside a clip.
- **The Trilemma shape:** the exact brand shape, never distorted, with video playing inside each wedge and no labels (section 3).

---

## 1. Lighting and colour standard (applies to every shot)

Every clip, whether stock or Higgsfield, is matched to this standard before editing, using one shared colour grade (LUT) plus per-shot corrections. Any clip that can't be matched is replaced, not forced.

| | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Used for** | People moments, the Trilemma shape, the resolve, the end card | "Systems underneath", Tria teams at work |
| **Key light** | Soft daylight from a large window, **camera left**, high and diffused | Soft, low practical light (screens, a lamp) at the subject's 3/4 angle |
| **Colour temperature** | Neutral-cool daylight, about 5,600K. Whites read clean white, never yellow. | Cool navy ambient. Skin is lit neutral-warm by screen light. |
| **Highlights** | Soft roll-off, nothing clipped. Windows glow but keep detail. | Very few. Screen glow plus the red trail only. |
| **Shadows** | Lifted, with a slight cool or navy tint (`#1D2038` direction). Never crushed black. | Deep navy `#111322`, still with detail. Never pure black. |
| **Contrast** | Medium-low, airy | Medium, cinematic |
| **Saturation** | Restrained. Navy, grey and white wardrobe and surroundings, with natural skin tones. | Low, except for skin and red |
| **Red** | Only from the red trail graphic and at most one small red object. No red walls or furniture. | The red trail is the only bright colour |
| **Atmosphere** | Slight bloom on windows | Light haze |
| **Lens / camera** | 35–50mm look, shallow depth of field, slow push-ins and pull-backs, locked or dolly moves only. No handheld. | Same |
| **Texture** | Fine film grain added over everything, so stock and AI clips sit together | Same |
| **Delivery** | 4K master (minimum 1080p source), 16:9, plus a 9:16 cutdown, 24 or 25 fps everywhere | Same |

**People and wardrobe:** diverse, believable, natural expressions with no posed smiles. Wardrobe in navy, grey, white and light blue. Nobody wears red, so the trail stays the only red (the stock veteran's red jacket would need recolouring or replacing).

**Set dressing, balanced:** each scene gets 2–3 specific details that make it feel real and nothing more. For example:
- **Home:** one plant, a few framed photos, a mug
- **Clinic:** a plant, a notice board in soft focus, a coat rack
- **Office:** a plant, notebooks, coffee cups

**Don't use:** visible brand logos (including laptop logos), readable screen text, flags, agency seals or insignia, sci-fi interfaces, or the CGI "AI look" (waxy skin, impossible hands, overly perfect surroundings).

## 2. The Tria Path (the red line), animated graphic

- **Look:** a wide band of several soft red strands (`#FF0045`, to be confirmed by marketing) with long-exposure motion blur and a gentle glow, like your corridor reference. Not a thin neon wire.
- **Continuity:** one line for the whole film. It leaves each shot at the edge of the frame and enters the next from the same side, so it carries the eye from cut to cut.
- **Timed to the voiceover:** it moves when key words land (cues marked ▶ in the tables below).
- **How it's made:** animated in editing over clean footage, with depth (it passes behind people and objects where possible). It costs no Higgsfield credits.

---

## 3. The Trilemma shape, motion rules

- It uses the exact geometry of `Trialama_shape.svg`, which is never scaled, warped or broken apart.
- Each wedge plays video: Access, Quality, Cost. There are no labels.
- Emphasis comes only from the video inside the wedges (slow push-ins), from brightness and colour, from the triangle edge glowing, and from the rings.
- The camera move through the centre triangle is the transition into and out of dark mode.
- Reference: `tria-trilemma-animatic-v2.mp4`

## 6. Production steps

1. **Pull stock footage** for every shot in sections 4 and 5. Check each clip against the lighting standard (section 1) and keep only clips that match.
2. **Fill gaps in Higgsfield** with short (4–6 s), simple shots: locked or slow camera, few people, no hands doing detailed work. Generate a still first, approve it, then animate it with sound off.
3. **Grade everything** with one shared grade (LUT) plus per-shot matching, then add grain.
4. **Build the motion graphics:** the Trilemma shape, the Tria Path, the on-screen lines in Lexend Deca, and the end card with the real logo file.
5. **Add the voiceover and music.** Light piano and strings, resolving on the logo.
6. **Export** a 4K/1080p 16:9 master and a 9:16 cutdown.
